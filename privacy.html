Privacy Policy

Proto XR ("we", "us") makes AIM XR. This policy explains what information we collect when you play, why, who receives it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

We collect what we need to run the game, keep matches fair and keep players safe. We do not sell your data.

What We Collect and Why

  • Your Meta account: Your Meta User ID, username and profile picture, to save your progress and show you on leaderboards.
  • Device and connection: Technical information such as your IP address, device identifiers and app version, to connect you to games, keep the game secure and prevent cheating.
  • Gameplay: Your stats and what you do in matches, to run the game, rank players and keep matches fair.
  • Voice chat: Your voice is sent live to the other players in your match, and may be recorded for safety reviews (see Voice Chat below).
  • Purchases: What you buy in the AIM XR store. Payment is handled by Meta; we never see your card details.
  • Reports, moderation and support: Reports made by or about you, moderation actions on your account, and messages you send to support.

Voice Chat

AIM XR has voice chat. Your voice is sent live to the other players in your match and is normally not stored.

When a player is reported or flagged for breaking our rules, we may save a short recording of that match, which can include the voices of the players in it, and an automatic transcript. We use it only to review that report.

You can mute any player and turn your microphone off in Settings at any time.

Fair Play and Safety

We use automated systems to detect cheating. When a player is reported or flagged, we may save a short recording of the match, including voice, so our moderators can review it.

Accounts banned for breaking our rules may be marked as banned on their public profile. If a player you reported is banned, we may let you know. We never tell anyone who reported them.

Automated Decisions

Some clear cases of cheating may lead to an automatic ban, decided by an automated review of the detection and the account's history. Clear cases of racism or hate speech in a reported player's voice chat may lead to an automatic voice mute. You can ask for any automated decision to be reviewed by a person: appeal at discord.aimxr.com or email privacy@protoxr.com.

Who Receives Your Data

We share data only with service providers that help us run AIM XR, and only for that purpose:

  • Meta, for sign-in and payments
  • Cloud hosting and storage providers
  • AI and moderation service providers

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect players and the game.

International Transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

How Long We Keep Data

We keep data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Match recordings are deleted within 90 days unless they are needed for a ban or an appeal. Moderation records are kept while the action applies and for a limited time after. Other account data is handled as described below.

Your Data Rights and Deletion Policy

You are in control of your data. We will delete your User Data in the following cases:

  • (a) By Your Request:You can request the deletion of your data at any time by sending an email to privacy@protoxr.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request." Please include your Meta User ID so we can identify your data.
  • (b) When You Leave Meta:If you delete your Meta account or revoke permissions for AIM XR from your Meta settings, Meta notifies us automatically. We will then automatically delete your data.
  • (c) When No Longer Needed:If an account is inactive for 24 months, we automatically delete the associated User Data from our systems.
  • (d) By Legal or Meta Requirement:We will delete your data if required by law or if we receive a formal request from Meta.

* Deletion is completed across our systems, including backups, within 120 days. We may keep records we need to enforce a ban or meet a legal obligation, only for as long as needed.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or receive a copy of your data, to object to how we use it, and to have a person review an automated decision. Email privacy@protoxr.com to use these rights.

Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

AIM XR is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
