AIM XR Rules

The rules you agree to before playing online. They apply in voice chat and everywhere else in the game.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The Rules

  • XNo N-word
  • XNo racism or hate speech
  • XNo bullying or harassment

Voice chat is moderated. Reports include what you said.

What Happens If You Break Them

Break a rule = muted, longer each time.

A voice mute lasts 1 day the first time, then 3 days, 1 week, 1 month and 3 months for each offense after that. While you are muted, other players can't hear you, but you can still play.

When a player is reported, we may save a short recording of the match with an automatic transcript so a moderator can check what was said. Clear cases of racism or hate speech may lead to an automatic mute.

Fair Play

Cheating and exploits are not allowed. Accounts caught cheating are banned, and the ban can be permanent.

Your Account Status

Open the HUB in the game menu to see any mutes or bans on your account, why they were given, when they end, and what you said in the reported clip.

Think We Got It Wrong?

A person can review any mute or ban, including automatic ones. Appeal on our Discord or email contact@protoxr.com.
